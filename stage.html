HOW TO WORK WITH USVIEW CAPABILITIES

WHERE WE ARE NOW

Three parallel tracks define the next phase.

The business does not need to wait for a complete proprietary aircraft before creating value. Engineering services, FOVEA maturation, and airborne prototyping can advance in parallel and reinforce one another.

01 // CONTRACT PERFORMANCE

Win the first bounded work and establish repeatable periods of performance.

We are actively pursuing first-pass government, prime-contractor, and industry work that is narrow enough to execute well and technical enough to demonstrate Peregrine's value.

  • RF / C5ISR engineering and troubleshooting
  • Systems integration and interface engineering
  • CAD, drawing packages, and technical data
  • Test, verification, sustainment, and field/depot support
  • Prototype engineering and documentation
02 // FOVEA MATURATION

Close defects, harden interfaces, and build qualification evidence.

FOVEA Engine and Peregrine Mission Console have reached an engineering-MVP baseline. The next software effort is less about adding flashy features and more about debugging, integration discipline, repeatable tests, representative inputs, and stable release behavior.

  • Defect closure and regression coverage
  • Representative hardware / network interfaces
  • Repeatable scenario and replay qualification
  • Operator workflow refinement
  • Hardware-in-the-loop readiness
03 // AIRBORNE PROTOTYPE

Find the right airframe partner and integrate before purpose-building.

Peregrine is looking for a prototyping / airframe partner that can provide a mature UAS or surrogate platform, practical integration support, and a credible path to repeated engineering flight activity.

  • Payload mounting and mechanical interfaces
  • Power / thermal / data integration
  • RF and antenna integration
  • Flight-test support and instrumentation
  • Feedback into future P-360 airframe requirements

HOW ORGANIZATIONS CAN SUPPORT THE NEXT STEP

Useful support is technical, contractual, or access-oriented.

The best help at this stage is not generic encouragement. It is a real problem, a bounded work package, access to a platform or test environment, an interface to integrate, or domain expertise that reduces a specific technical risk.

GOVERNMENT PROGRAM / DEPOT / LAB

Give us a scoped technical problem.

Small engineering tasks, sustainment gaps, technical-data needs, RF/C5ISR integration problems, test support, market research discussions, white-paper opportunities, and prototype evaluation contexts are immediately useful.

PRIME / SYSTEM INTEGRATOR

Give us defined workshare, not generic staffing.

We are best used where Peregrine can own an interface, subsystem analysis, integration package, drawing / technical-data effort, verification activity, or focused engineering problem with clear deliverables.

AIRFRAME / UAS COMPANY

Help us get the mission system airborne faster.

We are looking for a mature platform and engineering partner that can support payload integration, power/data/mechanical interfaces, antenna placement, flight-test planning, instrumentation, and repeated engineering sorties.

RESEARCH UNIVERSITY / LAB

Help independently attack hard technical risk.

Relevant areas include RF propagation, radar, signal processing, autonomy, tracking/fusion, controls, aero/structures, thermal systems, HIL/SIL, instrumentation, and independent validation. Peregrine is also exploring research engagement with Arizona State University; no formal university partnership is implied until a scope is executed.

COMPONENT / TECHNOLOGY SUPPLIER

Provide hardware we can integrate and measure.

Radios, SDRs, antennas, timing/navigation hardware, embedded compute, radar/sensors, data acquisition, power electronics, batteries, motors, test equipment, and ruggedized enclosures can all shorten the path to representative integration.

PROTOTYPE / MANUFACTURING SHOP

Turn engineering artifacts into test articles.

Composite and metallic fabrication, machining, harness fabrication, PCB assembly, fixtures, mounts, enclosures, structural coupons, and configuration-controlled drawing-to-hardware workflows are high-value partnerships.

NEXT MILESTONES

What progress should look like from here.

These are outcome gates, not marketing dates. The sequence is designed to reduce risk and create evidence before increasing technical ambition.

01

Execute first work

Deliver bounded engineering work under a documented period of performance and build referenceable execution history.

02

Stabilize FOVEA

Close remaining defects, expand regression / qualification evidence, and hold a stable engineering release baseline.

03

Select integration platform

Choose a surrogate airframe and lock mechanical, electrical, network, payload, and test interfaces.

04

Bench + HIL integration

Integrate representative radios/sensors/compute, exercise mission threads, instrument failure modes, and verify interfaces.

05

Flight-relevant demonstration

Move the mission system into repeated flight test, measure performance, identify constraints, and feed evidence back into PACN-01 and P-360.
